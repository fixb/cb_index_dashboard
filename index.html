FTSE Global Focus — Index rules

  • Conversion Premium ≤ 100%
  • CB Price > 60% and < 140%
Addition (inclusion rules)
  • Conversion Premium < 75%
  • CB Price > 70% and < 125%
  • Min size: US $500m · Europe €375m (≈$433m) · Asia ex-Japan $275m · Japan ¥22bn (≈$150m)

Assumptions & formulas

Per-bond P&L (in % of CB price)
  • Δ (equity) = Delta × Parity/CB × eq%
  • Γ (convexity) = ½ · Gamma · Parity/CB · (eq² + σ_idio²)
  • Vega = Vega / CB × ΔIV × 100
  • Rho = Rho(10bp) / CB × (Δrates/10) × 100
  • Credit = −Duration × ΔSpread / 100
Cross-sectional dispersion
  • Stock i return = market return + ε_i, β=1
  • From avg correlation ρ: σ_idio² = eq² · (1−ρ)/ρ
  • Gamma P&L gets a positive convexity boost from dispersion
  • Duration used as proxy for spread duration
  • Index aggregate weighted by FTSE Index Weight
